ISL v16 backlog test - PPK power capture summary
========================================================
Capture: 750,005 samples @ 10 ms = 7500 s (125.0 min)

*** MEASUREMENT CAVEAT - NOT a battery-life number ***
USB-C was attached for serial logging AND a battery was in series with
the PPK at the same time. With VBUS present the nRF52840 USB peripheral
stays clocked/enumerated, so the deep-sleep FLOOR reads ~1.67 mA here,
NOT the proven battery-only 34 uA (v7). USB also backfeeds/shares the
rail, so absolute currents are not deployment-representative. Also
SIM_FIX=1, so the periodic real-GPS burst is absent from this trace.
=> Use this capture for BEHAVIOURAL SHAPE only. For battery life, re-run
   battery-only (no USB), SIM_FIX=0, as the earlier clean PPK runs were.

Charge over the 125 min capture = 5.338 mAh
  ~88% at the (USB-inflated) floor; ~12% in BLE-settle / LoRa-TX-pass /
  boot GPS-time-sync bursts. No stuck-awake anomaly; every burst returns
  to the floor. The 42-packet backlog drain shows as a run of ~42 TX
  spikes ~15 s apart, each dropping back to the floor between (deep-sleep nap).
